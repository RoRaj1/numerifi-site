About Numerifi

Numerifi was founded by Rohan and Sung Woo, two high school students who noticed that financial literacy education hadn't caught up to the tools now shaping real markets. Most students graduate without ever opening a budgeting app, let alone understanding how AI is already reshaping investing and personal finance.

Numerifi exists to close that gap — giving students across the district a free, hands-on way to build real financial literacy and investing skills using the same category of AI tools used in the industry today.
